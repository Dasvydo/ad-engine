## Prompt

> Editorial still life for a marketing image, 1080x1350 4:5 portrait. Two printed emails lie side by side on a warm cream surface, clearly the same subject but different lengths and different paragraph shapes, the body text soft enough to read as texture rather than as words. Leave the top half clear cream for headline type; keep both sheets low, overlapping slightly. Soft natural daylight from above. Warm, low-contrast, editorial. Background is solid #FFF0E5 cream, evenly lit, no gradient, no texture noise. Warm neutral palette only, no blue, no cool grey, no neon. Do not render any text, any logo or any amber accent, those are typeset afterwards. Soft 16px rounded corners, low warm-tinted shadows, generous empty space. Calm, intelligent, unhurried. Not futuristic, not techy, not SaaS-hype.
